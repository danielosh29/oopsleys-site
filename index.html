Privacy policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what Oopsleys Animation Studio collects through oopsleys.com, why, who receives it, and how long we keep it. Oopsleys Animation Studio is a trade name of Danielle Design Plus LLC, a company in Florida, United States. Questions: hello@oopsleys.com.

What we collect

  • When you join the waitlist: your email address.
  • When you order: your name and email; your Instagram handle or business name; what you write in the form, such as the type of business, the occasion, who the video is for, your story, colors and notes; your logo and reference photos, if you add them; your answers about the people in the photos and the declarations you tick; and the language you used on the site.
  • Collected automatically with an order: your network address (IP address), the country and region it points to, and the time. We use them to prevent abuse, to keep a record of the declarations you made, and to refuse orders from places under comprehensive US sanctions.
  • When you pay: payment happens on our payment processor's page. We never see or store your card number. We receive the payment status and the email you used.
  • When you buy an extra round of changes: your order number, your email and the changes you describe.

How we use it

To make and deliver your video, to contact you about your order, to charge you once we confirm the order, to keep the records we need for accounting and the law, and to protect the site against abuse. If you joined the waitlist, we use your email to tell you when ordering opens. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Your photos and logo

  • Reference photos are used only to create the characters for your order. Location data is removed from them in your browser before they are sent.
  • Photos are never sent by email. They are kept in our order system and deleted from it automatically 30 days after your order is paid. Orders that are never paid are deleted, with their files, after 3 days.
  • To make your video, we may keep working copies on the studio's own devices and upload the photos to the AI animation tools we use. We delete our working copies, and the photos in our accounts on those tools, within the same 30 days.
  • The animation tools are run by other companies, which keep uploaded files under their own terms. Some of these companies state that they may use uploaded files to improve their AI models. We cannot delete what a tool keeps on its own servers.
  • Your logo is kept with your order so that we can make more videos for you. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • We never use a child's photo, or a video with a child's character, in advertising or as an example without written permission from the parent or guardian.

Who receives your information

We share information only with the services that run the studio, and only what each one needs:

  • Stripe, our payment processor, for payments.
  • Cloudflare, which runs our order system and stores orders and files, routes the studio's email, and provides our visit statistics.
  • Google, which hosts the studio's email inbox, where order details arrive, and provides the reCAPTCHA check on the waitlist form.
  • MailerLite, which stores the waitlist and sends our emails to it.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • The AI animation tools we use to make your video, which receive your story, logo and photos for that purpose and keep them under their own terms (see “Your photos and logo”).

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Visit statistics, cookies and your browser

We count how many times each page is opened, as one number per page per day, with nothing stored about the visitor. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which Cloudflare says works without cookies and without identifying visitors. We do not use advertising pixels or tracking cookies, and we do not track you across other websites. The site works the same whether or not your browser sends a “Do Not Track” signal.

This site does not set cookies. It remembers your language and accessibility choices in your own browser's storage, and you can clear them at any time. When you use the waitlist form, Google's reCAPTCHA check may set its own cookies. The payment page is run by Stripe on its own site.

How long we keep it

  • Orders that are never paid: deleted automatically, with their files, after 3 days.
  • Reference photos, of adults and of children: deleted automatically from our order system 30 days after the order is paid, and our working copies and the copies in our accounts on the animation tools within the same 30 days.
  • Your logo: kept with your order until you ask us to delete it.
  • Order details (what you wrote in the form, your network address and your declarations) and payment records: kept as long as we need them for accounting, tax and legal purposes. These details also reach the studio's email inbox with each order, without photos. Our payment processor keeps its own payment records under its own policy.
  • Your waitlist email: kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to hello@oopsleys.com. Every email we send to the waitlist has an unsubscribe link. We may need to keep some records, such as payment records, when the law requires it.

Children

Orders can be placed only by adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children. A character inspired by a child is made only when the child's parent or legal guardian places the order and confirms it in the form.

Where your information is processed

The studio is in the United States. Your information is processed in the United States and in the other countries where our service providers operate.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top.

Questions

Is there sound or dialogue?

There is no dialogue. That is the idea: the reel works in every language. Each video comes with its own background music and sound effects, created together with the animation. Tap the speaker on any video on this site to hear it.

Can I talk to someone first?

Yes. Email hello@oopsleys.com or message @theoopsleys on Instagram.

How are the reels made?

With AI animation tools, directed scene by scene.

Can I get a horizontal video?

Not at the moment. Every video is vertical (9:16), made for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. It is not a horizontal video for a regular YouTube upload or a TV screen.

Will the character look exactly like me?

The character is inspired by you and your features: your hair, your style and your general look, drawn in the Oopsleys style. It will remind people of you, but it will not look exactly like you.

Can my children appear in the video?

Yes, as cartoon characters inspired by them. Only a parent or legal guardian can order this, and confirms it in the form. We ask for everyday photos, make family scenes only, and delete the photos within 30 days at the latest.

Can another business order the same story?

Yes. The stories in our catalog are open to every customer, and each one is made again with that customer's logo or characters. For a story that is only yours, choose a new story.

Can I order a reel every month?

A monthly plan for businesses is coming. Message us if you want to be first.
